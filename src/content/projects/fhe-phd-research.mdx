## 01 Context

Modern computation increasingly happens on data you don't fully control — cloud servers, third-party ML pipelines, shared infrastructure. Fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) offers a way out: it lets you perform computations directly on encrypted data, producing an encrypted result that only the data owner can decrypt, without the party doing the computation ever seeing the plaintext.

## 02 Approach

My PhD research at NTNU, started in August 2026, focuses on privacy-preserving computation using FHE. The work is in its early stages — building up the theoretical and practical foundation before settling on a specific research question and methodology.
