## 01 Context

Verform's roof calculator — an internal tool used to price and configure roofing jobs — was running on an aging solution that needed a rebuild. As a consultant intern at Bouvet ASA over the summer of 2025, I was asked to lead a small intern team through modernizing it.

## 02 Approach

The first step was reading and understanding the existing solution and its documentation closely enough to reproduce its behaviour correctly. From there, the team rebuilt the calculator on a modern web stack. As team lead, I split the work by defining clear goals up front and assigning tasks based on each team member's strengths and areas for growth, rather than dividing work arbitrarily.

## 03 Result

The new roof calculator shipped as a working replacement for the legacy tool, and the internship doubled as a first real experience leading a small technical team — balancing hands-on implementation work with delegation and planning.
